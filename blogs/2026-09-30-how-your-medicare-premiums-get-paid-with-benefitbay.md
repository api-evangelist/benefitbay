How are my Medicare premiums paid through ICHRA (CHOICE)? After your Medicare coverage is active, benefitbay sets up direct premium payment from your ICHRA (CHOICE) account—so you do not pay first and wait to be reimbursed. Quick answer With benefitbay ICHRA (CHOICE), eligible Medicare plan premiums are paid directly from your ICHRA account once your coverage and payment setup are complete.
